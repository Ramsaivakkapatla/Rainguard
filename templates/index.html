HOW IT WORKS

From rainfall to payout

No lengthy claim forms. No waiting weeks for a surveyor.

01

Rainfall Measured

Independent rainfall sources report measurements for the farmer's region.

02

Data Verified

Multiple sources are compared to detect stale, missing or manipulated readings.

03

Policy Evaluated

The policy engine applies the configured rainfall rules.

04

Payout Settled

Eligible farmers receive the payout automatically, even offline.
